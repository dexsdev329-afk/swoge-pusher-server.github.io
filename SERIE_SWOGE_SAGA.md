# SWOGE SODA — SERIES BIBLE

*Serialized viral reboot. Built to beat the fruit show (300M).*

---

## 1. LOGLINE + EMOTIONAL THROUGHLINE

**Logline.** SWOGE — a hyper-muscular shiba-inu from 2099 — teleports into the cramped Paris apartment of LEO, his broke, deadpan 25-year-old owner. In SWOGE's future, Leo is about to make ONE catastrophic choice that erases SWOGE from existence. SWOGE came back to stop it. The problem: he has no idea *which* of Leo's dumb little daily decisions is the fatal one. And he's running out of time — his body is flickering.

**The emotional throughline (why people care, not just laugh).**
This is not a dog trying to save the world. It's a dog trying to save a *friendship* — his own. In 2099, SWOGE and Leo are inseparable; SWOGE is the best thing that ever happened to a lonely guy. If the timeline breaks, SWOGE doesn't die dramatically — he just *never gets adopted*, never meets Leo, and the version of Leo who was loved by this dog simply never exists. SWOGE is fighting to protect the day they met, which hasn't happened yet.

Present-day Leo doesn't know any of this. To Leo, a jacked time-traveling dog is yelling vague warnings about his grocery choices. The audience knows the stakes; Leo doesn't; SWOGE *can't explain them clearly* (future privacy rules? paradox? he just can't). That gap — the audience aching for Leo to understand what this dog would do for him — is the engine. People laugh at the absurdity and stay for the ache.

**One line that holds the whole show:** *"I crossed seventy years to find you, and I can't even tell you why."*

---

## 2. THE CLOCK / FLICKER MECHANIC

SWOGE's body glitches **electric-blue** — a stutter, a half-frame of static, like a signal dropping. This is the clock. It is the show's heartbeat and its stakes made visible with zero on-screen text.

**The rule:** the flicker tracks how close the *bad* timeline is.
- **Calm (safe):** solid, warm, present. Maybe one soft shimmer a scene — a reminder, not an alarm.
- **Stressed (fork approaching):** rhythmic pulse, blue edge-glow, voice briefly doubles/echoes on a flicker.
- **Critical (bad choice looming or made):** rapid stutter, limbs lag a frame behind, parts of him go translucent, audio tears.
- **The brink (season-level):** he can't hold a solid form for more than a second. Played dead straight. Not scary — *sad*.

**What makes it WORSE (escalate):**
- Leo moving toward the fatal decision.
- SWOGE using future-tech or over-explaining (burns timeline energy — his own warnings cost him).
- Time simply passing. The default drift is downward.

**What makes it BETTER (relief — this is the reward beat):**
- Leo making a choice that, by luck or love, bends away from the bad path. The flicker *settles*. SWOGE exhales. The audience exhales.
- A moment of genuine connection between them — a shared laugh, Leo scratching his ears without thinking. Connection literally stabilizes him. **The bond is the cure.** This is the thesis made mechanical.

**Why it's shootable:** flicker is a Veo-friendly visual effect layered on a locked reference character — electric-blue glitch, translucency, audio stutter. No text. Reads instantly in 9:16 even muted (the glitch *is* the caption).

---

## 3. THE VOTING MECHANIC (the participation engine)

Every episode ends on a **real fork**: Leo must choose **A or B**. Both look trivial. Neither Leo nor the audience knows which one kills SWOGE.

**The ask.** SWOGE turns to camera (he's the only one who can — Leo can't see the "lens") and, in his deep rugged voice, asks viewers to **vote in the comments**: *"A or B. Choose for him. I can't."* Short, 4th-wall, under 8s.

**The payoff.** Next episode **opens by reading the winning side** — SWOGE "hears" the comments as a blue rush of voices, reacts to a couple of them out loud ("*'Pick B, you buff idiot' — fine. B.*"), and we **shoot that timeline.** The crowd's choice is canon.

**How votes visibly change things (stakes you can SEE):**
- **The flicker reacts to the vote.** A good collective call → SWOGE stabilizes on screen, color returns. A bad one → he stutters harder, something in the apartment glitches with him.
- **The apartment ages with the timeline.** Each bad fork, the set subtly degrades toward the empty-future version: a photo frame goes blank, a plant wilts, BRENDA's lights dim. Each good fork, warmth creeps back. Viewers are literally redecorating Leo's life.
- **The counter is SWOGE's body.** We never show vote tallies on screen (no text). The *number of good forks the audience has banked* is read off how solid SWOGE is. Comment-section lore: "we got him back to 80% last week, don't blow it."

**Why this beats the fruit show's version:** the fruit show votes on who gets eliminated — external, cruel, repeatable. We vote to save a character who is *begging us by name*, and the result is painted onto his body and his home. The audience isn't picking a loser; they're the co-owners of the dog.

## 4. THE EPISODE FORMULA (repeatable, ~60–80s = 8–10 clips of ~8s)

Every episode is the same skeleton. Familiar format = shareable format.

1. **HOOK (0–2s) — the 2-second rule.** Open mid-chaos or mid-stakes, never on setup. SWOGE flickering hard, or a cold line to camera: *"This fridge is trying to kill me."* No logo, no runway. The first frame must make the thumb stop.
2. **THE RECAP-BY-REACTION (clip 1–2).** SWOGE reads the winning comments from last episode, we jump into the chosen timeline. Doubles as "previously on" for new viewers without text.
3. **RISING STAKES MIDDLE (clips 3–6).** Leo does something mundane. SWOGE is sure it's The Decision — and is comically, confidently wrong with useless future-advice. The flicker worsens. Comedy and dread climb together. BRENDA needles from the wall.
4. **THE TURN (clip 7).** The *actual* fork reveals itself — and it's smaller and dumber than the thing SWOGE was panicking about. Stomach-drop: *this* is the one.
5. **THE FORK (clip 8).** Leo freezes between A and B. Hand hovering. SWOGE realizes he can't choose for him and can't explain.
6. **VOTE-TO-CAMERA (clip 9).** SWOGE breaks the wall. Deep, quiet: *"A or B. Choose for him. I can't."* Flicker spikes on the last word.
7. **CLIFFHANGER + LOOP (final beat).** Hard cut on Leo's hand / a bigger glitch / BRENDA's smug line. The last frame rhymes with the first so autoplay loops seamlessly. Unresolved. Come back tomorrow.

**Cadence:** post daily or near-daily. The vote closes overnight; the next episode is the reveal. The rhythm *is* the retention.

---

## 5. CHARACTER ARCS ACROSS A SEASON

**SWOGE** — deep, rugged, warm underneath. Starts as a cocky time-cop who thinks saving Leo is a quick fix and he'll be home by dinner. Arc: the more he fails to "fix" Leo with logic, the more he learns the timeline doesn't bend to information — it bends to *connection*. He stops trying to command Leo and starts trying to *know* him. By the finale he understands the fatal choice was never a grocery item; it was a moment Leo almost gave up on himself. SWOGE's win isn't a corrected timeline — it's becoming, in the present, the friend he already was in the future.

**LEO** — dry, flat, broke, quietly lonely. Starts treating SWOGE as a hallucination / nuisance / his weirdest roommate yet. Arc: from annoyed, to reluctantly attached, to — without ever being told the stakes — choosing, at the brink, the thing that keeps the dog solid, because he's started to care about this ridiculous animal. Leo never gets the full truth. He just learns he'd rather have the dog around. That's the tearjerker: he saves SWOGE by accident, out of love he can't name.

**BRENDA** — the passive-aggressive smart fridge. Comic antagonist, secret oracle. Starts as pure sass ("*I've logged this choice. For the records.*"). Arc: she's been online since before SWOGE arrived — she has *data* on Leo. Midseason reveal: BRENDA has quietly been nudging Leo toward good forks all along, in her insufferable way, because a fridge gets attached too. She's the only one besides SWOGE who remembers the forks. Her deadpan sabotage of SWOGE's ego is the running comedy; her loyalty is the surprise.

**The odd-couple deepening:** Season tracks a thaw. Early episodes: SWOGE vs. Leo, Leo vs. the dog, BRENDA vs. everyone. Late episodes: the three of them vs. the timeline, bickering like a family. The flicker stabilizing over the season is the visual proof the bond is working.

## 6. RUNNING MOTIFS & QUOTABLE CATCHPHRASES

Lines are short, English, repeatable — bait for the comments.

- **SWOGE:** *"A or B. Choose for him. I can't."* (the vote tag — every episode)
- **SWOGE:** *"That's not the decision. ...Is it the decision?"* (his signature wrongness)
- **SWOGE:** *"In 2099, you don't do this."* (useless future-advice runner)
- **SWOGE (soft, on a flicker):** *"I crossed seventy years to find you."*
- **LEO (flat):** *"My dog is from the future and he's an idiot."*
- **LEO:** *"Cool. Okay. Normal morning."*
- **BRENDA:** *"I've logged this. For the records."*
- **BRENDA:** *"Low on milk. And judgment."*
- **The paw, never a hand** — SWOGE reaching for things with paws is a running sight gag and a lore rule (he's a *dog*).
- **The empty photo frame** — degrades/refills with the timeline; fans watch it.
- **The flicker on the last word of every vote** — a visual signature, like a logo sting.
- **BRENDA's dimming lights** = the scoreboard nobody announces.

---

## 7. WHY EACH CHOICE DRIVES RETENTION / SHARES

The fruit show proved five things: serialized rising stakes, a participatory vote + comments read aloud, a locked recognizable format, absurdity played dead straight, and cliffhangers. We hard-code all five and fix our old show's fatal flaw — the weekly reset. The **flicker** turns invisible stakes into a muted-friendly visual clock, so a scrolling thumb reads "this character is dying, slowly" in one frame (retention). The **vote** makes viewers co-authors begging to be begged — and because the result is painted onto SWOGE's body and Leo's apartment, people return to see *what they did*, and comment to recruit others to their side (shares + a self-replenishing comment war). Reading winning comments aloud rewards participation and doubles as a text-free "previously on" (new-viewer onramp). The **friendship-at-stake** core gives the absurdity a heart, so clips get sent with "this made me cry about a buff cartoon dog," which travels further than a laugh. The **fixed format + daily cliffhanger** builds appointment habit. And the **bond-as-cure** mechanic means the emotional payoff and the visual payoff are the same event — the audience's love literally makes the dog solid.

## PRODUCTION RULES (every script obeys)

- **Characters locked by reference image.** SWOGE = furry buff shiba, black gym shorts, **paws never hands, always a dog, never human.** Leo and BRENDA per their refs.
- **Clips max 8 seconds.** Episode = 8–10 clips. Design beats to cut clean at 8s.
- **All dialogue English and SHORT** — must fit ~8s spoken. One or two lines per clip, max.
- **No on-screen text / subtitles** (they render as garbage). Stakes carry through the flicker, the set, and BRENDA's lights.
- **Vertical 9:16.** Frame for faces and the flicker; last frame rhymes with first for the loop.
- **Fixed voices:** SWOGE deep/rugged, Leo dry/flat, BRENDA passive-aggressive.
- **Avoid real public figures and filter-risky words.** Keep it clean, keep it shootable.
- **The vote tag and a flicker beat appear in every single episode** — that's the format spine; don't drop them.

# SWOGE SODA — Serialized Saga (Episodes 1-3)

**Series bible (quick reference for the shoot):**
SWOGE is a hyper-muscular buff shiba-inu from 2099 — fully furry, black gym shorts, DOG PAWS NEVER HANDS, deep rugged voice. He teleports into LEO's cramped Paris apartment. In the real future SWOGE is Leo's beloved dog and best friend, but Leo is about to make ONE choice that erases SWOGE from existence. SWOGE came back to stop it — but he can't tell WHICH choice is the fatal one. His body FLICKERS electric-blue, worse as the bad timeline nears. BRENDA is a passive-aggressive smart fridge. Comedy from his useless future-logic. Drama from the clock and the bond.

Format rules honored throughout: clips max 8s, English dialogue, short lines, no on-screen text, vertical 9:16. Every episode opens by "reading" the top vote/comment from the previous one, and ends on a 4th-wall VOTE line where the flicker reacts.

## EP 1 — "THE ARRIVAL"

- **Logline:** A jacked future dog crashes into a broke Parisian's kitchen to stop a choice that will erase him — but he has no idea which choice it is.
- **Hook (first 2s):** Extreme close-up on a dog paw slamming flat onto a cheap IKEA table, electric-blue sparks crawling up the fur. SWOGE (deep, rugged): "Leo. Don't move."
- **The stake this episode:** Leo learns a buff future dog exists, loves him, and will cease to exist unless they identify and stop Leo's fatal mistake — starting tonight.
- **The fork (A vs B):** Leo's phone buzzes with two things at once — a job rejection he wants to rage-reply to, and an unknown number. **A: fire off the angry reply.** **B: answer the unknown call.** SWOGE flickers at BOTH, which terrifies him.

### Shots

**1. Camera — Snap-zoom (table level up to full room)**
*Video prompt:* Dim Paris studio, one warm lamp. Blue light EXPLODES from a spark the size of a fist; buff shiba-inu SWOGE materializes mid-crouch on the kitchen table — fully furry, black gym shorts, paws flat on the wood (never hands). His whole body FLICKERS electric-blue for half a second, then solid. Dishes rattle.
SWOGE: "Two thousand ninety-nine. I had three seconds. I used them."
LEO (offscreen, flat): "...You're on my table."

**2. Camera — Low-angle hero shot**
*Video prompt:* From floor level looking up: SWOGE rises to full height, massive, backlit by the fridge light, paws flexing. Steam of blue static rolls off his shoulders. LEO stands in frame edge in a hoodie, unimpressed.
SWOGE: "In my time, you're my best friend."

SWOGE: "You will. Unless you ruin it tonight."

**3. Camera — Over-the-shoulder (Leo's POV onto SWOGE)**
*Video prompt:* Over Leo's shoulder; SWOGE paces the tiny kitchen, too big for it, knocking a mug with a paw. He points a single claw at Leo. No flicker yet — he's steady, confident.

SWOGE (beat): "...That's the part I don't have."

**5. Camera — Push-in on SWOGE's face**

SWOGE: "That's me dying, Leo. Slowly."

**6. Camera — Insert (extreme close-up on phone screen)**

SWOGE: "Which one were you about to touch?"

**7. Camera — Whip-pan between Leo and SWOGE**
*Video prompt:* Fast whip-pan from Leo's thumb hovering over the screen to SWOGE — who FLICKERS HARD, both arms glitching blue, staggering into the counter. Then whip back to Leo. Then back to SWOGE — still flickering. Panic on the dog's face.
SWOGE: "No— wait—"
LEO: "What?"
SWOGE: "You flickered me on BOTH. Both choices hurt."

**8. Camera — Two-shot, handheld**
*Video prompt:* Handheld, both in frame, cramped kitchen. SWOGE grabs the table edge with both paws to steady himself; the blue is pulsing like a slow heartbeat now. LEO lowers the phone an inch, finally unsettled.
LEO: "So one of these ends you."
SWOGE: "Or neither. Or both. I came back blind, Leo."

**9. Camera — Low push-in, hero framing**
*Video prompt:* Camera glides up to SWOGE at full height, paws planted, blue flicker rolling across his fur in waves timed to his breath. He turns slowly to the lens. The apartment goes quiet.

**10. Camera — Extreme close-up, SWOGE to lens (4th wall)**

## EP 2 — "THE WALLET"

- **Logline:** Leo's dead $4.12 crypto wallet becomes a life-or-death puzzle when SWOGE flickers the instant Leo reaches for it.
- **Hook (first 2s):** Extreme close-up on SWOGE's paw holding Leo's phone up to the lens like evidence, blue static licking the screen edge. SWOGE (to camera): "Top comment said A. So Leo sent the angry reply. Now watch what it unlocked."
- **The stake this episode:** The angry reply (chosen by the audience) bounced back a weird auto-draft that mentions money Leo forgot he had — a locked wallet with $4.12 in it and a recovery choice attached. SWOGE suspects the wallet is the fatal thread.
- **The fork (A vs B):** The wallet offers two paths. **A: drain it now** — swap the $4.12 for cash tonight. **B: leave it locked** and walk away. SWOGE flickers violently on one of them — but the clock is making his readings unreliable.

LEO (offscreen): "And nothing happened."
SWOGE: "Something happened. Open your wallet."

### Posting mechanics

- **Native 9:16, no borders, no on-screen text/subtitles.** Export clean verticals per platform (TikTok, Reels, Shorts); never post with a visible crop or a watermark from another app. Dialogue carries everything — keep lines tight so they land inside each ~8s shot.
- **First-2s rule:** Every episode opens on the single strongest image (cracked-screen reflection EP4, 3:00 clock + blue glow on the floor EP5, SWOGE dissolving mid-shout EP6). No logo card, no "previously on." The hook IS the retention.
- **Loop:** End each episode on the vote line, then hard-cut back to a frame that rhymes with the opening (EP6's self-lighting phone loops to EP1's arrival). A clean loop boosts watch-time and tricks the second view.
- **Cadence:** Post EP4→5→6 about 48h apart — long enough for a vote to accumulate, short enough that the flicker-clock urgency stays alive. Tease the drop time in the pinned comment ("next one when A or B hits ___ likes").
- **Reading comments on camera next episode:** Open every episode with SWOGE physically holding the phone (paws, never hands — rest it on a paw or pin it to the counter) and reading the literal top comment aloud, then SHOW that timeline in a 1-second flash before the story continues. This rewards commenters (their words appear on screen), proves the votes are real, and forces people to comment THIS time so they get read NEXT time.
- **How to run the vote:** Pinned comment with "❤️ this comment for A / ❤️ the reply for B" (like-to-vote is friction-free and visible), PLUS count "A"/"B" replies as backup. Announce the winning count at the top of the next episode as canon. Keep the two options to a hard binary — never three — so the comment section splits cleanly into two warring camps.
- **Series-binding:** Pin EP1 in comments ("start here"), add all parts to one TikTok Playlist / Reels series / Shorts "episodes" tab, and end captions with "EP4/6", "EP5/6", "FINALE 6/6". Reply to top comments with "ep X is pinned" to funnel binge-watching. Consistent cold-open format (read comment → flash timeline → story → fork → vote) trains the audience to expect the ritual.
- **Duet/Stitch template:** Explicitly invite it — "Stitch this with YOUR vote and why" and "Duet the dissolve and react."
